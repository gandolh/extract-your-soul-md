# McAdams' narrative identity

**Verdict: *kept*** — the single highest-leverage prompt in the whole design.

## The finding

People structure their life stories with either **redemptive sequences**
(bad → growth) or **contamination sequences** (good → ruined). This structural
choice predicts **agentive vs. passive sentence construction across *all* their
writing** — not just when they describe adversity.

That generality is what makes it so valuable: one well-asked life-story prompt
("tell me about a time something went wrong and what came of it") captures more
global voice signal than a 60-item Likert scale, because the *shape* of the
answer leaks into the shape of everything they write.

## How it's used

Q4 in [[../../02-questionnaire-design]]. The extractor reads not just the
content but the **arc** (redemption/contamination) and the **agency**
(does the narrator act, or get acted upon?).
