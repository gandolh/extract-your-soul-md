# MBTI & 16personalities (NERIS)

**Verdict: *borrowed*** — never scored by us; used only as a *vocabulary* for
one optional self-knowledge question (Q11).

## The honest science

- **Poor test-retest**: ~50% of takers get a different 4-letter type on retest
  (Pittenger 1993/2005).
- **False dichotomies**: underlying distributions are unimodal, not bimodal;
  most people sit near the midpoint where one point flips a letter.
- **Weak predictive validity**: Stein & Swan 2019, Adam Grant ("astrology for
  nerds").
- **16personalities/NERIS** adds an Assertive/Turbulent axis that is just Big
  Five Neuroticism inverted, then relabels types with friendly names.

## Why it's still useful for *this* tool

Despite the science, the N/S, T/F, A/T axes *do* map to observable writing
differences — abstract vs. concrete, logic-framed vs. relationship-framed,
confident vs. hedged. And the vocabulary is widely understood, so it works as a
self-report *prompt*: "if you've taken this, what felt right?" (Q11). The
"what felt right" half is the signal; the test name is noise.
