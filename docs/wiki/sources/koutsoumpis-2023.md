# Koutsoumpis et al. (2023) — kernel of truth in text-based personality

**Link**: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/369321862
**Type**: meta-analysis (n = 85,724 across 31 studies)

## Takeaways

- Self-report Big Five ↔ LIWC linguistic style: |ρ| = .08–.14 (~5% variance).
- Observer-report from text ↔ Big Five: |ρ| = .18–.39 (~38% variance).
- The asymmetry is the empirical basis for preferring observed voice and
  open-ended samples over self-report instruments.
