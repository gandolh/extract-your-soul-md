# Zero-Shot GenAI Scoring of Brief Open-Ended Text (2025)

**Link**: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12974486/ (Nature Human
Behaviour)

## Takeaways

- LLMs scoring Big Five from brief open-ended narratives converge with
  self-report at rs = 0.30-0.53 — comparable to or exceeding established
  psychometric benchmarks, and **outperforming LIWC dictionary methods**.
- Spontaneous narrative text, not structured answers, is the richest source.

## Why it matters

**Confirms and quantifies** the project's core assumption that open-ended
written responses beat Likert scales for voice/trait elicitation. No
contradicting evidence found in the 2024-2026 sweep.
