# Individual Turing Test (2026)

**Link**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01289

## Takeaways

- No current method passes the Individual Turing Test when judged by people who
  *know* the target person — they detect artificiality. The same simulations
  fool strangers.
- Fine-tuning captures everyday style best; RAG/memory captures opinions and
  preferences best — complementary techniques.

## Implication for this project

Sets a realistic ceiling: a `my-soul.md`-driven voice will read as plausibly
"this person" to outsiders, but close acquaintances remain the hardest judges.
Matches the intended use case (humanize/consistent AI text) more than
impersonation.
