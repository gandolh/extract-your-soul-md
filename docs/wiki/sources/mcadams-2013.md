# McAdams (2013) — Personality, Modernity, and the Storied Self

**Link**: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0963721413475622
**Type**: framework paper

## Takeaways

- Life stories take redemptive (bad→growth) or contamination (good→ruined)
  shapes.
- That structural choice predicts agentive vs. passive construction across all
  of a person's writing — not just adversity narratives.
