## Big Five / Five Factor Model

- [Koutsoumpis et al. (2023) — The kernel of truth in text-based personality
  assessment: A meta-analysis of Big Five × LIWC](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/369321862)
  — The single most-cited paper in this design. Establishes that
  self-report Big Five correlates only weakly (~5% variance) with
  linguistic style, while observer-report from text is much stronger
  (~38%). n = 85,724 across 31 studies.
- [Yarkoni (2010) — Personality in 100,000 Words](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2885844/)
  — Large-corpus blog analysis identifying per-trait linguistic
  signatures (Openness = formal/intellectual vocabulary, etc.).
- [Mairesse et al. (2007) — Linguistic cues for automatic personality
  recognition](https://users.soe.ucsc.edu/~maw/papers/personality_jair07.pdf)
- [Pennebaker — The Secret Life of Pronouns](https://www.secretlifeofpronouns.com/)
  — Function-word personality signals; first-person singular research.
- [Costa & McCrae (1995) — Domains and Facets](http://jenni.uchicago.edu/econ-psych-traits/CostaMcCrae1995.pdf)
  — NEO-PI-R structure.
- [Soto & John (2017) — The Next Big Five Inventory (BFI-2)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/295563312)
- [BFI-2 materials — Colby Personality Lab](https://www.colby.edu/academics/departments-and-programs/psychology/research-opportunities/personality-lab/the-bfi-2/)
- [BFI-2-S and BFI-2-XS (short forms)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0092656616301325)
- [Berkeley Personality Lab BFI page](https://www.ocf.berkeley.edu/~johnlab/bfi.html)
- [TIPI — Gosling Lab](https://gosling.psy.utexas.edu/scales-weve-developed/ten-item-personality-measure-tipi/)
  — Free 10-item Big Five instrument; 18 language validations.
- [TIPI 18-language scoping review (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10330951/)
- [IPIP — public domain item pool](https://ipip.ori.org/)
- [IPIP-NEO-120 facet items](https://ipip.ori.org/30FacetNEO-PI-RItems.htm)
- [NEO PI-R Wikipedia overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revised_NEO_Personality_Inventory)
