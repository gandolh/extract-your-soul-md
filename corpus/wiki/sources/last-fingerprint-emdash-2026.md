# The Last Fingerprint (2026) — How Markdown Training Shapes LLM Prose

**Link**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27006

## Takeaways

- The em dash is "markdown leaking into prose" — a residue of the
  markdown-saturated training corpus.
- Em-dash frequency persists even under explicit suppression instructions
  (0.0/1k words in Llama up to 9.1 in GPT-4.1 *under suppression*).
- Reframes em-dash frequency as a diagnostic of fine-tuning methodology, not a
  stylistic choice — i.e. a robust, hard-to-suppress tell.
