# CharacterBot (2025) — Beyond Profile: Deep Persona Simulation

**Link**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12988

## Takeaways

- Fine-tuning on biographical facts yields surface imitation but misses deeper
  voice; "holistic representation goes beyond surface-level facts."
- Uses a multi-task objective (linguistic structure + 3 fine-tuning tasks) and
  a CharLoRA mechanism blending a general style expert with task experts.
