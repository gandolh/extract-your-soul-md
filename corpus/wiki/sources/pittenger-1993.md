# Pittenger (1993) — Cautionary Comments on the MBTI

**Link**: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/232494957
**Type**: critique

## Takeaways

- ~50% of takers get a different 4-letter type on retest.
- Underlying score distributions are unimodal, not the bimodal split the
  dichotomies assume.
