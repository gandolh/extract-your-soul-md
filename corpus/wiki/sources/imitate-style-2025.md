# How Well Do LLMs Imitate Human Writing Style? (2025)

**Link**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24930 (IEEE UEMCON)

## Takeaways

- Few-shot prompting: up to 23.5× higher style-matching accuracy than
  zero-shot; completion prompting reaches ~99.9% on surface-style metrics.
- Prompting strategy matters more than model size.
- But matched outputs have lower perplexity (15.2) than human essays (29.5) —
  stylistically similar yet statistically *more predictable*: a detectable AI
  tell.
