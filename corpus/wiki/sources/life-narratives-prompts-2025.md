# Life Narratives & the Ten Aspects of the Big Five (2025)

**Link**: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jopy.70080 (Journal
of Personality)

## Takeaways

- Broad open-ended prompts ("Who are you?") capture wide personality signal;
  **targeted event-specific prompts** (high-point / low-point / turning-point)
  better isolate specific trait facets (e.g. agency, communion).
- Narrative agency correlates with Extraversion-Assertiveness.

## Implication for questionnaire design

A *design nuance* (not a contradiction): the `--interview` / web question set
already includes one event-specific prompt (Q4, narrative identity), which this
supports. Could justify adding a second targeted prompt (e.g. a high-point) to
sharpen facet signal. ⚠️ Revisit in [Questionnaire Design](../sources-raw/02-questionnaire-design.md).
