# Wikipedia: Signs of AI Writing

**Link**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing
**Type**: continuously-maintained practitioner catalogue

## Takeaways

The most comprehensive crowd-maintained catalogue of AI tells. Categories:

- **AI vocabulary**: delve, tapestry, pivotal, underscore, foster, crucial,
  intricate, landscape.
- **Structural**: rule of three, "not X but Y", tailing participle clauses,
  numbered-list inflation.
- **Copula avoidance**: "serves as" replacing "is".
- **Tone/format**: promotional tone, title case, excessive bold, markdown
  characters bleeding into plain prose, sales-copy em-dash placement.

The "tailing clause" pattern (participles tacked on to add vague significance)
is documented here and rarely elsewhere.
