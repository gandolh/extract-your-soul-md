# Frontiers (2022) — explainable personality from open-ended interviews

**Link**: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.865841/full
**Type**: empirical study

## Takeaways

- Three to five open-ended questions outperform structured questionnaires for
  predicting language behavior.
- The free-text response is simultaneously self-report and language sample.
