# Enneagram

**Verdict: *borrowed*** — we use one core-fear/core-want *prompt* (Q9). We do
**not** assign a type.

## The science

Psychometrically weak: a 2020 systematic review of 104 samples found "mixed
evidence of reliability and validity"; factor analyses recover fewer than nine
factors. Often called pseudoscientific.

## Why one prompt survives

The Enneagram's *narrative vocabulary* — every type defined by a **core fear**
and a **core desire** — is richer than any trait model for explaining *why*
someone writes the way they do. The pseudoscience is irrelevant because we
borrow only the question shape:

> "I most want to be seen as ____, and I most fear being seen as ____."

Two blanks, two sentences of the person's own writing, an enormous amount of
voice color and motivational signal that no trait scale captures. See Q9 in
[Questionnaire Design](../sources-raw/02-questionnaire-design.md).
