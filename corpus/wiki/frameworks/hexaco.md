# HEXACO

**Verdict: *borrowed*** — we keep the Honesty-Humility *intuition* and probe it
inside the values/belief questions (Q3); no separate scored block.

## What it adds over Big Five

A sixth factor: **Honesty-Humility** — sincerity, fairness, greed-avoidance,
modesty. It cleanly isolates the moral-behavioral cluster that Big Five
collapses into noisy low-Agreeableness.

## Why it matters for *voice*

H captures whether someone writes with **self-promotion or self-deprecation**,
**status-signaling or modesty** — a real, observable register dimension that
Big Five mostly misses. We watch for it when reading the belief-asymmetry
answer: does the person frame a contrarian belief with humility or with
grandiosity?

Instruments (unused here): HEXACO-PI-R, public-domain IPIP-HEXACO-60.
