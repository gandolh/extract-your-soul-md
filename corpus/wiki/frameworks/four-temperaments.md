# Four Temperaments (Hippocrates / Galen / Keirsey)

**Verdict: *dropped*** — no unique signal; of historical interest only.

Sanguine, Choleric, Melancholic, Phlegmatic. Formalized by Keirsey (1978).
Mathematically equivalent to the Extraversion × Neuroticism plane of the
[Big Five](big-five.md) with only four buckets — too coarse to be useful directly.
Anything it would tell us is already captured, with more resolution, by the
Big Five linguistic correlates and the open-ended prompts.
